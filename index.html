JOURNAL
Published · Systems and Computing (SyCom) Vol. 2, No. 2 · Jul 2026

Revisiting Classical and Deep Learning Models for Multi-Class Brain MRI Classification under Severe Data Constraints

With Bekir Karlik. Evaluates classical machine learning (LBP+KNN, GLCM+KNN, SVM, MLP) against a lightweight CNN and a ResNet-50 transfer-learning model for five-class brain MRI classification — Alzheimer's, ischemic stroke, meningitis, brain tumor, and normal — under a small, heterogeneous dataset. Finds that SVM's accuracy is partly a product of dataset bias, CNNs generalize poorly despite exploiting spatial features, and ResNet-50 saturates early, underscoring the gap between model scale and available data in constrained biomedical imaging settings.
